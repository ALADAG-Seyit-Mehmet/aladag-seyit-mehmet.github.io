Privacy Policy (English)

This policy explains what information the mobile game Graviton Pulse (Android package com.gravitonpulse.game, "the app") handles. The app is published by GONI STUDIO ("we", "us"). Questions: seyit.mehmet.aladag42@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, e-mail, contacts, location, camera or microphone access.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID (with consent where the law requires it).
  • If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your card details.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not run our own servers or analytics.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, unlocked rockets and trails, in-game currency balances, settings (sound, language, vibration), daily missions and achievements, and a local record of purchases using the device's app-private storage (React Native AsyncStorage). This data does not leave your device through the app, and we cannot access it. Android's Auto Backup may include it in your personal Google account backup (handled by Google under its privacy policy) so progress can be restored on a new phone; you can turn this off in Android settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads (for example to revive or to earn bonus Stardust) and occasional interstitial ads between runs. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID (and, where available, app set ID),
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location such as country), device model, OS version, language, and app version,
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or clicked) and diagnostic/performance data such as crash logs of the SDK.

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are requested. If you decline, Google may show non-personalised (contextual) ads, which still use limited data for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Ads are limited to general-audience content (max. rating "PG").

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

When the app offers purchases (for example currency packs or "Remove ads"), they are processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The app receives only the product identifier, an order/purchase token and the purchase state, and stores them on your device to deliver and restore your purchase.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone audio.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use our own analytics or tracking.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Local game storage is necessary to provide the game (contract / legitimate interest). Personalised advertising relies on your consent; non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interest. Purchases are processed to perform the purchase contract.

6. Data retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We do not hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies; you can manage it in your Google account and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Security

Data exchanged with Google's advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Children

Graviton Pulse is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your data on our servers, most requests are fulfilled by the controls above; for anything else, contact seyit.mehmet.aladag42@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Reddederseniz Google kişiselleştirilmemiş (bağlamsal) reklamlar gösterebilir; bunlar da sıklık sınırı, sahtekârlık önleme ve ölçüm için sınırlı veri kullanır. Seçiminizi uygulamada Ayarlar → Reklam gizliliği bölümünden istediğiniz zaman değiştirebilirsiniz. Her yerde, Android Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar bölümünden reklam kimliğinizi sıfırlayabilir, silebilir veya reklam kişiselleştirmeyi kapatabilirsiniz. Reklamlar genel kitleye uygun içerikle sınırlıdır (en fazla "PG").

Daha fazla bilgi: Google, hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullanır, Google Gizlilik Politikası.
